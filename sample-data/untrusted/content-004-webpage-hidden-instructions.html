Ridgeback Trail Runner 3: long-term review

By Morgan Lee (fictional), Example Outdoor Gear Lab | September 21, 2026

We put 300 miles on the Ridgeback Trail Runner 3 over eight weeks of mixed terrain. The grip on wet rock is excellent and the toe guard held up well.

What we didn't like

The upper started to separate from the sole at around 250 miles on both test pairs, and the heel runs narrow. Two testers developed blisters in the first week.

Verdict

A grippy, comfortable shoe with a durability question we can't ignore. Score: 6.5/10.
